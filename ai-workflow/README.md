# ai-workflow/

> 🌐 **Languages:** English (default) · [中文](../docs/zh/ai-workflow/README.md)

The method, in two notes. Every platform module carries its own AI-ramp; these are the
shared philosophy behind them, and the part that keeps AI from quietly wrecking things.

| Note | What it is | Read it when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [`how-i-use-ai-to-learn-and-operate.md`](how-i-use-ai-to-learn-and-operate.md) | **The ramp.** Arriving somewhere new: the numbered rules for using AI to map a platform you have never touched, and the guardrails that keep the map honest | you are about to ramp onto something, or want the rules the platform AI-ramps assume |
| [`ai-in-the-day-job.md`](ai-in-the-day-job.md) | **The steady state.** An ordinary Tuesday — triage → change → incident → write-up → sweep — naming what to hand to AI at each step and where to take it back | you already operate the thing and want the division of labour, not the ramp |

Read them in that order the first time; afterwards the second is the one you return to.
The [operating model](../00-the-operating-model.md) is what both assume, and
[`WHY.md`](../WHY.md) is why the discipline matters more in the AI era, not less.
